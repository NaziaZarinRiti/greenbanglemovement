Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience to volunteer?

No. You don’t need to be an expert. Our team will show you what to do at nursery and planting days. All you need is the willingness to learn and show up.

Where does GBM work?

GBM is based at the Asian University for Women in Chattogram. Our main planting site is the coastal village of Guliakhali in Sitakunda Upazila, and we also plant on hillsides around Chattogram.

How can I donate?

Email us at greenbangleproject@auw.edu.bd or use the form on this page, and we’ll send you our bank and mobile banking details.

Can my organisation partner with GBM?

Yes. We work with institutions, collectives and allies on research, materials, transport, training and funding. Choose “Partner with GBM” in the form and tell us what you have in mind.
