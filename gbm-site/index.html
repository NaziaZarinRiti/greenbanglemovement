Who we are

Green Bangle Movement (GBM) is a student-led climate initiative at the Asian University for Women in Chattogram. It began in 2019, when students planted palms around the AUW campus as a natural fence. Today we work with coastal women in Guliakhali, Sitakunda, on nurseries, mangroves and palmyra palms.

The name comes from the bangle that women in Bangladesh wear every day. Our green bangles are rings of trees along the coast, planted and cared for by the women who live there.
