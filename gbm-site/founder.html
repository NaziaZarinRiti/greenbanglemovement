Our founder

The chemist and educator who started the Green Bangle Movement.

  • RoleFounder and Lead Mentor, Green Bangle Movement
  • PositionDirector, Environmental Sciences Program, Asian University for Women
  • Based inChattogram, Bangladesh

Dr. Mosae Selvakumar Paulraj

Also known as Dr. P. Mosae Selvakumar

Dr. Mosae Selvakumar Paulraj is a chemist and educator at the Asian University for Women (AUW) in Chattogram, where he directs the Environmental Sciences Program. He began the Green Bangle Movement in 2019 with a small group of students planting palms around the AUW campus, and has since grown it into a funded coastal restoration project working with women in Guliakhali, Sitakunda.

His work brings together green chemistry, traditional ecological knowledge and community action. A major focus is palmyraculture: developing value-added products from the Asian palmyra palm that can create income for rural women while the trees help protect the coast.

“A bangle is never a single strand — it is many hands, closing a circle of care around the land.”

Dr. Mosae Selvakumar Paulraj
