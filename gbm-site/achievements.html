Achievements

Awards, innovations and progress since GBM began in 2019.

November 2025 · Bandarban

National biodiversity recognition

Honoured at the Bangladesh BioDiversity Summit 2025 for involving women in forest restoration, coastal resilience, biodiversity documentation and grassroots awareness.

International

Honoured for palmyra research

The Green Bangle Project was honoured at the International Symposium for Environmental Innovation and Palmyraculture Research.

August 2025 · AUW

International climate conference

Co-organised ICECA 2025, the International Conference on Environment and Climate Action, with AUW’s Environmental Sciences Program.

August 2025 · ICECA 2025

Best Presentation award

The student team behind the Green Bangle EcoMeter app won Best Presentation at the International Conference on Environment and Climate Action.

Innovation

Green Bangle EcoMeter app

Built by AUW computer science students Purnata Biswas, Subaita Shirin and Tahiyat Islam, the app helps people track and reduce their carbon footprint. It is on Google Play.

Chattogram city

City planting drives

With Chattogram City Corporation, YouthNet Global and local schools, hundreds of palmyra seeds were sown along the Patenga airport road.

Partnerships

Funding and research partners

Funded by the Swiss Philanthropy Foundation, with agreements with YPSA and the Bangladesh Sugarcrop Research Institute for planting techniques and research.

Guliakhali, Sitakunda

Coastal planting

Thousands of mangrove and palmyra saplings planted along the Guliakhali shoreline, working towards the goal of about 30,000 trees.

Women and livelihoods

Training for coastal women

Women from Guliakhali trained in home nurseries and organic fertiliser, and now work as paid caretakers and make Keora pickle, palm foods and handicrafts.
